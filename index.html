04 / OUR MISSION

Explore the past.
Build the future.

The Digital Frontier History Lab connects middle school Texas History and U.S. History with immersive technology and student-led historical inquiry.

Exhibits currently shown are AI-generated historical reconstructions for educational demonstration. They are interpretations, not original historical photographs or primary-source evidence. Interactive scenes are hosted by ThingLink and require an internet connection and viewing permissions.
